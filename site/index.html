Put your Mac in overdrive.

More than 70 small tools macOS is missing — for Finder, windows, the Dock, the keyboard, the screen and the clipboard. Turn on what you need, give it a shortcut, forget about it.

Everything macOS forgot.

Each tool is off until you turn it on, and every one can have a global shortcut.

Finder

Cut and paste files, Return to open, Delete to trash, ⌥N for a new text file, and a context menu with 13 actions.

Windows & Dock

Hide or minimize everything, a green button that fills the screen, click a Dock icon to hide its app.

Apps

⌘Q twice to quit, quit all apps, hide inactive ones, install from disk images, a shortcut for any app.

Keyboard & mouse

Hyper key on Caps Lock, no Caps Lock delay, middle click with three fingers, Delete to go back in Safari.

Screen

Copy text from anywhere on screen, read QR codes and barcodes, pick colors in Hex, RGB, HSL or OKLCH.

And much more

Clipboard clearing, Downloads tidying, screenshots, Dark Mode and Night Shift, keep awake, Cleaning Mode…

Native

Written in Swift. A light menu bar app with the look of macOS.

Private

Everything happens on your Mac: no account, no tracking. Free and open source (MIT).

Always up to date

New versions are offered automatically and install in one click.

Floor it.

Free, for macOS 14 Sonoma or later. If macOS blocks the app on first launch: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway.
